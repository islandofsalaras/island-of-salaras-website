Privacy Policy for Island of Salaras Beta

Effective Date: 21 October 2025

Island of Salaras, values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information while you participate in the beta test of our game.

1. Information Collection

During the beta test, we do not collect any personal data from players. All game data, settings, and progress are stored locally on your device.

2. Use of Information

Since no personal or identifiable data is collected, we do not use your information for any purpose.

3. Third-Party Services

The beta version of the Game does not integrate with third-party services that collect user data.

4. Security

All data stored locally on your device is your responsibility. We recommend following standard device security practices to protect your data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game develops. Beta testers will be notified of significant changes before they take effect.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at salarasstudio@gmail.com.
